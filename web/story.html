02How it works

Five steps from scrolling to wearing.

i

Show us your best fits

Drop in five photos of outfits you feel great in. The AI reads your aesthetic, how you dress and what you reach for.

ii

Get your colour palette

You choose your undertone, and it builds a palette to explore, a starting point and never a rule. Every card tells you why it was picked.

iii

Swipe like you mean it

Right to like, left to pass, down to save. Pass on something and it never comes back. Like it and you get more in that spirit.

Explore offers, finish the look

Reference pieces explain your style direction. Live shopping results show observed prices; compare offers and explore complementary categories.
